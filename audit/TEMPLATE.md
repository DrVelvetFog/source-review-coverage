## 5. Findings

One per item. Severity is about *what the evidence shows*, not about what it might mean.

### F-1 — *title*

- **Evidence.** What was observed, with the tier: *ran* / *read* / *told* / *recalled* /
  *inferred*.
- **Where.** Commit, file, run, or journal line.
- **What it means.** Stated as a consequence of the evidence, no further.
- **What would close it.** The change, and how the auditor would recompute that it is closed.

## 6. What this audit does not establish

A pass establishes review coverage and nothing else: not correctness, not that a human
read anything, not truthful authorship, not reviewer independence, not existence at a
time, not compliance.

In addition, for this audit: the sampled range is the most recent *N* commits of each
kind, not all history; a residual is unreviewed content, not necessarily wrong content;
the agent-action journal covers only sessions the journal recorded; and nothing here is a
legal opinion or a certificate of meeting any obligation. Where a section is empty, the
auditor had no evidence, and an empty section is never a pass.
